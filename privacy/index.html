Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

We collect as little as we can, use it only to tell you about Umbrella Days, and delete it when you ask.

Who we are

Umbrella Days is an independent UK brand. We're responsible for the details you give us on umbrelladays.com. You can reach us at hello@umbrelladays.com.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we store:

  • your email address
  • any optional answers you give: how many doors you'd want, your Kallax colour, your door colour and your country
  • which website or link brought you to us, if your browser tells us
  • the date you signed up

We don't use cookies, tracking pixels or analytics on this site.

How we use it

  • To email you about Umbrella Days, including your 15% off code when doors go on sale.
  • To decide which colours to make, how many, and where to ship first.

We use your details because you've asked to join the waitlist (your consent). You can withdraw that consent at any time. We won't sell your details or share them with anyone to market their own products.

Where it's stored

Waitlist details are stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in London. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, processes your IP address to deliver the page. Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until you ask us to remove them or we stop working on Umbrella Days, whichever comes first.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to stop emailing you. Every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe, or just email hello@umbrelladays.com.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
