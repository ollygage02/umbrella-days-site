Made for rainy days indoors.

Umbrella Days makes things for the hobbies you enjoy when it's raining outside. We're starting with records.

Why record doors

A Kallax is where most record collections end up. The cubes are the perfect size for LPs, but your best covers turn into a row of spines, and the open shelves gather dust.

So we're making doors for it. Each one frames a record on the front and protects the rest behind it, in every Kallax finish and a few bold colours of our own.

Where we're at

We're an independent UK brand, finalising the design before our first production run. The waitlist tells us which colours and how many doors to make first.
