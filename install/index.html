How to fit your doors

Five steps, one drill. Each door fixes inside a Kallax cube with two hinges and four screws.

You'll need

  • A drill
  • A screwdriver, or a screwdriver bit for your drill
  • A pencil

In the box

  • Your door, with both hinges already fitted
  • A drilling template
  • 4 screws
  • An acrylic front pane

  1. Choose a side

    Hinges go on the left or right wall of the cube. Hinges on the left means the door opens from the right, and the other way round.

  2. Mark the holes

    Hold the template flush against the front edge of your chosen wall. Mark the four holes with a pencil.

  3. Drill pilot holes

    Drill a small pilot hole at each mark. This stops the board splitting when the screws go in.

  4. Screw in the door

    The hinges come fitted to the door. Line them up with the pilot holes and drive in the four screws.

  5. Add your record

    Slide any 12-inch sleeve into the front frame, with the acrylic pane in front to keep dust and UV off the cover.
